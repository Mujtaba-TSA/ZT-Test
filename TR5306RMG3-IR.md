## Key features
- **FFP RMG 3 Illuminated reticle (glass-etched, patented)** — designed to maximise speed and accuracy, with patented features that let shooters quickly estimate range to targets and read wind-speed and drop-correction information for fast, accurate second-shot corrections; push-button digital illumination for low light. RMG 2 Illuminated and TREMOR3 Illuminated are also available.
- **5–30x magnification with 56mm objective and Japanese ED glass** — outstanding light transmission and clarity at high and low power, machined in Japan.
- **34mm one-piece tube** — generous internal adjustment (35 MRAD elevation, 15 MRAD windage) and rigidity.
- **Precision Turret System (PTS) with zero stop** — oversized, tactile turrets in 0.1 MRAD (10 mils/rev) with a reliable, easily set zero stop and confident return to zero.
- **Capped windage turret with hidden-thread design** — a clean profile that keeps threads protected without separate thread protectors.
- **Side parallax (20 m to infinity)** with extended distance markings, **removable throw lever**, and a **locking dioptre**.
- **Tenebraex Tactical Tough flip-up covers** and a **56mm sunshade** — protection and glare control.
- **Argon purged, Weathershield lens coating, IPX7 waterproof, made in Japan**; illuminated model; backed by ZeroTech's lifetime guarantee.

## Video insights
Reviews of the Japanese-made Trace Advanced 5-30x56 (this SKU uses the RMG 3 reticle) present it as a major step up for the brand into ELR and PRS:
- **Build:** A large, overbuilt 34mm tube with a 56mm objective for maximum light transmission; premium fit and finish, with ED glass, argon purging, and Weathershield coatings.
- **Turrets:** Oversized, easy-to-read windage and elevation turrets with strong tactile clicks, 0.1 mil increments and 10 mils per revolution, a reliable zero stop, and a capped windage turret; around 35 mils (~120 MOA) of elevation is available, with the option to remove the zero stop to extend elevation (reducing windage).
- **Reticle and features:** The patented RMG 3 supports range estimation, wind reads, and fast second-shot corrections (RMG 2 and TREMOR3 are alternatives); push-button illumination with multiple brightness settings, a removable/adjustable throw lever, a locking dioptre, screw-in lens covers, and parallax from 20 m to infinity with extended markings.
- **Performance:** In field testing the platform tracked accurately and held centre from 300 m out to around 1,200 m, handled PRS-style dialling and holdovers, and performed strongly in low light.
- **Verdict:** Outstanding tracking, precision, and reliability — an excellent, aggressively engineered choice for PRS and long-range shooters.

## Manual
Full setup, care, warranty, and safety instructions are in the shared **Trace / Trace Advanced manual reference** (`_Trace_manual.md`) in this folder. Sections that apply to this model:
- **Turret zero / zero stop:** Elevation zero stop set via 2mm hex grub screws (orange zero-stop ring); capped windage turret (hidden-thread) re-indexed to '0' via its three 2mm grub screws
- **Turret index:** 0.1 MRAD — 1cm at 100 metres (0.36" at 100 yards) per click (10 mils per revolution)
- **Illumination:** Push-button digital — 10 brightness settings, CR2032 battery (~2-hour auto-off, resumes previous setting)
- **Parallax:** Side focus — 20m (22yd) to infinity
- **Dioptre:** Locking — turn the lock ring clockwise to secure the setting
- **Lens covers / sunshade:** Tenebraex Tactical Tough flip-up covers; 56mm sunshade included
